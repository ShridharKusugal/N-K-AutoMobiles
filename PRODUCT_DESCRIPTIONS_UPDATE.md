## 🔍 Example: Before vs. After

### BEFORE (Product ID 1 - Honda Shine Piston Kit)
```
Original Honda Piston Kit for Shine 125cc
```

### AFTER
```
Original Honda Piston Kit for Shine 125cc motorcycles. This genuine OEM 
replacement piston kit ensures optimal engine performance and longevity. 
Manufactured with high-grade aluminum alloy, it features precision-machined 
surfaces for perfect cylinder fit. The kit includes piston, piston rings, 
gudgeon pin, and circlips. Designed to withstand high temperatures and 
pressures, providing excellent compression and fuel efficiency. Compatible 
with Honda Shine, Shine SP, and CB Shine models. Recommended replacement 
at 40,000-50,000 km for maintaining peak engine performance.
```
